---
title: Devices
description: Send all traffic of one device through the tunnel, or leave a device out entirely.
---

The **Devices** tab is per-app routing from the phone, for whole devices on the network.

| Mode | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Everything through the tunnel** | all the device's traffic goes through Auto, even in "by lists" mode. Handy for a TV or a set-top box |
| **Nothing through the tunnel** | the device is left out entirely, even in "all traffic" mode. Handy for a work computer or banking apps |

Direct rules still apply to "everything through the tunnel" devices: Russian sites stay direct if
that preset is on, for example.

A device is chosen by its IP address; the list suggests the DHCP leases. Give the device a static
lease (**Network → DHCP and DNS → Static Leases**), or it may get another address after reconnecting.
