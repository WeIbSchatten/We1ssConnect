A rule says **what** (services, domains, subnets) goes **where**. Rules are checked from top to
bottom and the first match wins; drag rows to change the order.

## Where to send

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Through the tunnel** | through Auto, a server group or one server |
| **Through a router interface** | through a VPN already set up on the router: WireGuard, AmneziaWG, OpenVPN and others |
| **Directly** | around the tunnel; addresses come from the DNS for direct sites (Yandex by default) |
| **Block** | the site does not open; the DNS query is refused |

## What a rule can match

- **Services** — community lists from
  [itdoginfo/allow-domains](https://github.com/itdoginfo/allow-domains): YouTube, Telegram, Discord,
  Meta, X, TikTok, Google AI, "Blocked in Russia", "Russian services from abroad" and more. They
  refresh themselves. For services whose apps connect by IP (Telegram, Discord, Meta, X, Cloudflare
  and others) the router loads their subnets too.
- **Presets** — "Russian sites and IPs" (`.ru`, `.su`, `.рф`, the `category-ru` list and Russian IP
  ranges) and "Ads and trackers" (`category-ads-all`). The same lists as in the app.
- **Categories** — `geosite:…` and `geoip:…`, as in Xray and v2rayN (see below).
- **Domains** — one per line; a domain also covers its subdomains.
- **IP addresses and subnets** — such as `149.154.160.0/20`.
- **Remote lists** — URLs of sing-box rule-sets (`.srs`, `.json`) or plain text lists of domains and
  subnets. Lines like `example.com`, `*.example.com`, `domain:example.com`,
  `DOMAIN-SUFFIX,example.com`, addresses, subnets and `#` comments are understood.

## Categories

Categories are the ones of Xray's `geosite.dat` and `geoip.dat`: `geosite:ru-blocked`, `geosite:youtube`,
`geosite:category-ads-all`, `geoip:ru`, `geoip:telegram` and hundreds more. sing-box on the router does
not read `.dat`, so the router takes the same data from
[runetfreedom/russia-v2ray-rules-dat](https://github.com/runetfreedom/russia-v2ray-rules-dat), which publishes
every category as its own `.srs` rule-set, and keeps them up to date. These are the same files the phone
and computer apps use, so the names match.

Write categories in the **Categories** field or right in **Domains** and **IP addresses and subnets**.
Categories with attributes (`geosite:google@cn`) are not supported. A category that does not exist is
skipped with a warning on the overview. Subnets of `geoip:` categories in "through the tunnel" rules are
captured too, so apps that connect by IP also go through the tunnel.

## How it works

In **by lists** mode the router answers DNS queries for domains of "through the tunnel" rules with
service addresses from `198.18.0.0/15` (FakeIP). Connections to them and to the rules' subnets are
captured and handed to sing-box, which knows which domain stands behind which address. All other
traffic never touches sing-box, so the router spends nothing on it.

In **all traffic** mode everything except the local network is captured, and the domain is read
from the connection itself.

## Server groups

A group is a small Auto of its own over some servers: only Finnish ones, say. Point a rule at the
group and that service always goes through one country. A group takes the servers of the chosen
subscriptions and the ones you tick, optionally filtered by name with a regular expression
(`finland|helsinki`).

## Lists and GitHub

Lists download from GitHub once a day (change it in the settings). If your ISP blocks GitHub, turn on
**Settings → Lists and subscriptions → Download lists through the tunnel**.
